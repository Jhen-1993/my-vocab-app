## WordNet part-of-speech reference

`data/parts-of-speech.js` was produced by cross-referencing the app's English headwords with the noun, verb, adjective, and adverb indexes from WordNet 3.1. The app distributes only its own compact part-of-speech labels; it does not include WordNet definitions, synsets, examples, or database files. Business compounds and learning phrases not present in the index were manually tagged from their card meaning and example context.
